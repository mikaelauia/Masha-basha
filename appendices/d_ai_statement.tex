\chapter{AI statement}\label{app:ai}
Following IKT466 guidelines on artificial intelligence tools, we document all AI systems used and our verification procedures.

\paragraph{Tools used.}
OpenAI Codex (GPT-6.1 Terra and GPT-6.1 Sol) assisted with planning, implementation, debugging, experiment execution, analysis, plotting, and initial drafting. Anthropic Claude provided editorial critique during intermediate reviews, and Google Antigravity (Gemini 4 Argon and Gemini 4 Carbon) assisted with structural refinement, prose editing, specification and code alignment checks, and final proofreading.

\paragraph{Verification.}
All architectural descriptions, equations, and hyperparameters were verified directly against the Python source code, and all metrics, per-topic $F_1$ scores, bootstrap intervals, parameter counts, and timings were checked against saved run logs. For example, the seed-42 Subword CNN confusion matrix (Table~\ref{tab:confusion-review}), topic-level net correctness changes (Table~\ref{tab:topic-correct-change}), and paired flip counts (Figure~\ref{fig:paired-changes}) were independently recomputed from stored article-level predictions.

\paragraph{Author responsibility.}
The authors assume full responsibility for all submitted code, experimental results, references, and claims, and are prepared to explain every component of the work.
