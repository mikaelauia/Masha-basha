\section{Model settings}
Table~\ref{tab:app-hyperparams} summarizes preprocessing, architecture, and optimization settings across the shared 20,000-article training and 4,000-article validation splits.

\begin{table}[!htbp]
\centering\footnotesize
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{35mm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X@{}}
\toprule
Pipeline component & Configuration details \\
\midrule
Shared preprocessing & Concatenate title and description, lowercase, extract \artifact{[a-z]+} tokens, and cap at 128 words. Filter exact normalized overlaps and label conflicts. \\
Training arms & Clean training uses 20,000 uncorrupted articles (5,000 per topic), and augmented training replaces 50\% per topic (10,000 total) in place at $p = 10\%$ swaps (static per seed). \\
\textit{Word TF-IDF LR} & Unigrams and bigrams ($n \in \{1, 2\}$), $\mathrm{min\_df} = 2$, $\mathrm{max\_features} = 50{,}000$, sublinear TF $(1 + \log f_{td})$, smoothed IDF, and $L_2$ row norm. Multinomial $L_2$ logistic regression via L-BFGS ($\le 1{,}000$ iter.), $C \in \{0.5, 2.0, 8.0\}$ tuned on combined validation Macro-F1 ($C = 2.0$ clean, $C = 8.0$ augmented). \\
\textit{Word CNN} & 30,000-row vocabulary (29,998 words, pad ID 0, \texttt{<unk>} ID 1), 96-dim $\mathcal{N}(0,1)$ embeddings, parallel 1D convolutions (64 filters each, $k \in \{3, 4, 5\}$, $\operatorname{ReLU}$), masked global max pooling (192 features), dropout $0.5$, and linear head ($192 \to 4$). Cross-entropy, AdamW ($\mathrm{lr} = 10^{-3}$, weight decay $10^{-4}$), batch 128, gradient clipping at 5.0, $\le 8$ epochs (patience 2). \\
\textit{Scheduled Word CNN} & Seed-42 schedule control using \textit{Word CNN}'s architecture (2,954,692 scalars), trained for $\le 16$ epochs with patience 4 and \texttt{ReduceLROnPlateau} halving LR after 2 stalled validation epochs. \\
\textit{Subword CNN} & Same 16-epoch schedule plus $8{,}193 \times 96$ fragment table ($+786{,}528$ scalars, 3,741,220 total). Uses \texttt{<} and \texttt{>} boundary markers, 3 to 5 character n-grams, and $\le 16$ evenly spaced fragments hashed via 64-bit \texttt{BLAKE2b} into $1 \dots 8{,}192$, averaged with \texttt{EmbeddingBag} and added to each word embedding before convolution. \\
\bottomrule
