\section{Hyperparameter configurations}
Table~\ref{tab:app-hyperparams} summarizes the complete hyperparameter and architectural specifications of the models analyzed in the main text. All models share the exact same alphabetic tokenizer (\artifact{[a-z]+}), limit of 128 words, training split of 20,000 articles, and validation split of 4,000 articles.

\centering\footnotesize

\toprule
Pipeline component & Configuration details \\
\midrule
Shared preprocessing & Concatenate title and description, lowercase, extract \artifact{[a-z]+} tokens, retain the first 128 words. Filter normalized text for exact overlaps and label conflicts. \\
Training arms & \textbf{Clean:} 20,000 uncorrupted training articles (5,000 per topic). \textbf{Augmented:} 50\% of each topic (10,000 articles total) replaced in place with versions generated at the interior letter swap target $p = 10\%$ (static per fitting seed). \\
Word TF-IDF LR & Word unigrams and bigrams ($n \in \{1, 2\}$), $\mathrm{min\_df} = 2$, $\mathrm{max\_features} = 50{,}000$, sublinear TF $(1 + \log f_{td})$, smoothed IDF, $L_2$ row normalization. Multinomial logistic regression with $L_2$ penalty, L-BFGS ($\le 1{,}000$ iterations), tuned over $C \in \{0.5, 2.0, 8.0\}$ on validation $J(f)$ ($C = 2.0$ selected for clean, $C = 8.0$ for augmented). \\
Word CNN & Word vocabulary with 30,000 rows (29,998 words + pad ID 0 + \texttt{<unk>} ID 1), embeddings of dimension 96 initialized from $\mathcal{N}(0,1)$. Parallel 1D convolutions use 64 filters each at kernel widths $k \in \{3, 4, 5\}$ and $\operatorname{ReLU}$. Global max pooling with padding masked (192 features) is followed by dropout $0.5$ and a linear head ($192 \to 4$). Cross-entropy loss, AdamW ($\mathrm{lr} = 10^{-3}$, weight decay $10^{-4}$), batch size 128, gradient norm clipping at 5.0. Up to 8 epochs with early stopping patience 2 on $J(f)$. \\
Scheduled Word CNN & Identical architecture and parameter count (2,954,692 scalars) to Word CNN, trained for up to 16 epochs with early stopping patience 4 and a \texttt{ReduceLROnPlateau} scheduler that halves the learning rate after 2 epochs without improvement in $J(f)$. \\
Subword CNN & Extends Scheduled Word CNN (16 max epochs, patience 4, plateau LR halving) with an 8,193 $\times$ 96 character fragment embedding table (786,528 added scalars, 3,741,220 total). Adds boundary markers (\texttt{<}, \texttt{>}) before extracting character n-grams of lengths 3 to 5, keeps up to 16 evenly spaced fragments per word, hashes each into $1 \dots 8{,}192$, and adds the fragment mean to the word embedding prior to convolution. \\
